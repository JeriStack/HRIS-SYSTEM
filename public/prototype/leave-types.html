Leave Policies & Category Management

Configure statutory leave entitlements, annual credit quotas, and eligibility parameters.

Approved System Business Rules: Leave requests are calculated strictly on Monday–Friday business days (Saturday and Sunday are automatically excluded). Modifying default quotas applies to future annual credit provisions without retroactively modifying existing active balance balances.

📋 Standard Corporate Leave Entitlements

5 Active Categories
ID Leave Type Name Default Annual Quota Description & Policy Intent Calculation Rule Status Actions
1 Vacation Leave 15 Working Days Annual personal rest, rest, and leisure. Subject to HR operational clearance. Monday–Friday (Excl. Weekends) Active
2 Sick Leave 10 Working Days Medical illness, medical consults, or recuperation. Medical certificate required for > 2 days. Monday–Friday (Excl. Weekends) Active
3 Emergency Leave 5 Working Days Unforeseen family emergencies, bereavement, or sudden domestic crises. Monday–Friday (Excl. Weekends) Active
4 Maternity Leave 60 Calendar Days Statutory childbirth and postpartum convalescence benefit for eligible female staff. Statutory Calendar Duration Active
5 Paternity Leave 7 Working Days Statutory paternity leave granting male employees time off to assist their spouse during delivery. Monday–Friday (Excl. Weekends) Active

📅 Working Days Only

The system calculation algorithm strictly counts Monday through Friday. If an employee applies for Friday through Monday, the duration equals 2 working days.

⚖ Strict Deduction

Balance deduction occurs in MySQL only when an application is Approved. Rejected or Cancelled requests never deduct leave credits.

🔒 Scope Boundaries

In accordance with approved specifications, complex unapproved policies such as carry-forward rollover and monthly proration are not implemented.
